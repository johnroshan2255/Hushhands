HUSHHANDS: sounds and music
===========================

Every recorded file in this folder is CC0 1.0 (public domain): free for personal and commercial use,
no credit required. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (The same files Fogbash uses.)
Most sound effects in the game (footsteps, creaks, squeaks, barks, snores, whistles) are synthesised
in the browser and are not files at all.
